Only needed once on each device. It stays saved in this browser, and anyone without it can't add anything.

How to make the key
  1. Open github.com/settings/personal-access-tokens/new.
  2. Name it "ARMORY", set an expiration you're happy with.
  3. Repository access: Only select repositories → Painfully-Hueman-Website.
  4. Permissions → Repository → Contents: Read and write.
  5. Generate, copy, paste it above.

Photos

The best 8 seconds become the animated cover: the most replayed part when YouTube has that, otherwise the part with the most movement. Maker and name are filled in from the channel and title if you leave them empty. Links are fetched by the studio Mac, so they go through while it's on and awake.

The first photo is the cover. Automatic: backgrounds are cut out, paintings are cropped to the canvas, books keep the whole photo. You can change it later with Redo photos. Videos (up to 70 MB) become an animated cover: an 8-second silent loop, like a GIF. The full video and its sound are not put online.
